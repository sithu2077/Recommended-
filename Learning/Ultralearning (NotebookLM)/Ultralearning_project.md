# Ultralearning Project

## Step 1

- Do your research.
- What topic you're going to learn and its approximate scope (start with a narrow scope, which can be expand as proceed).
- The primary resources you're going to use (books, tutorials, courses, ...).
- A benchmark for how others have successfully learned this skill or subject.
- Direct practice activities.
- Backup materials and drills.

## Step 2

- Schedule your time (test your schedule for one week before you commit to it).

## Step 3

- Execute your plan (with 9 principles of Ultralearning).

## Step 4

- Review your results (replicate successful projects / success is the best teacher).

## Step 5

- Choose to maintain / Master what you learned.

## Options

1. Maintenance.
2. Relearning.
3. Mastery.
